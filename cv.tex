\section{Research Interests}
My research interests center on integrating Remote Sensing, GIS, and GeoAI technologies for multimodal, explicit geospatial and environmental modeling, with a focus on heterogeneous, human–nature coupled landscapes. I am particularly interested in analyzing ecosystem services and examining how diverse landscape features interact to shape environmental and social resilience.
